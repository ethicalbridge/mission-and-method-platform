Included with your course

One Excel workbook for every module.

Each module of the Planning System has its own workbook, with one sheet per lesson. Use it offline, share it with colleagues or keep a version history. Workbooks are part of the paid course at no additional cost; they do not require a software subscription.

Sheets named after an Impact Tool (for example Roles, Indicators, Risks or Tasks) use that tool’s columns, so you can import the workbook into the tool later without retyping. The workbooks are being prepared; downloads open to course purchasers once each file is versioned and access is verified.

B3 · Deep

Organisational therapy.

Change management, behavioural change, leadership · ~5–6 months, phased

Different from B1 and B2. Here we're not building new systems onto a blank page; we're changing how the organisation thinks, decides and leads. For teams at an inflection point — a new strategic direction, a leadership transition, a crisis that revealed structural gaps, or a scale-up that outgrew the original setup.

  • Deep assessment: interviews with team, volunteers, leaders, board and key partners
  • Surveys that surface what's spoken and unspoken, with the diagnosis shared back honestly
  • Change plan with named owners, dates and a check-in rhythm
  • Leadership coaching for the lead(s)
  • Roles and routines redesigned with the team, each role trained on the new tools
  • Optional on-site missions (travel costs discussed up front)
  • Six months of the Impact Tools after handover + mission playbook + 6-month scale-up plan

Good when the organisation knows what it wants to become but needs help getting the team, routines and leadership to actually become that.

What I don't do

Clear limits, so expectations are honest.

Scope is as important as scope. These are the lines I don't cross.

  • I start from what you bring. You bring the idea or the organisation; I turn it into something you can run. If you're still deciding what to build, you need a different kind of advisor.
  • I'm not part of your team. I prepare, review and coach, but the outputs are owned, held and run by the organisation. The aim is capability, not dependence.
  • I don't promise funding. No donor, platform or grant round can be guaranteed. I prepare you well; the decision stays with the funder.
  • I don't commit your funds, sign contracts or speak publicly in your name.
  • Nothing provided is legal advice. No lawyer-client relationship is formed. For jurisdiction-specific matters, engage counsel licensed where you operate.
  • I don't run operations day-to-day. Running the organisation is the team's job; I help build what makes that running possible.
How we work

Practical, collaborative, no dependence.

01 · We agree in writing

Before any paid engagement starts, we sign a one-page working agreement: what I lead, what you decide, what I never do. No surprises later.

02 · I do the work; you own it

I draft, prepare and coordinate so the work doesn't fall on you. Every output has a named owner on your side from week one.

03 · Short cycles, honest feedback

I share drafts early and ask for sign-off within the window we agreed in the Terms of Reference. If something isn't working, I say so the same day and we adjust.

04 · Timelines stay fixed when yours do

I plan every date to be kept. That depends on your team responding on time. If delays start to pile up, we shrink scope rather than move dates.

05 · We leave a handover, not a cliff

Every engagement ends with the handover we agreed in the Terms of Reference. Typically a named owner on your side for each output, a short guide, and a plan for how your team keeps running it alone.

06 · Fees reflect the work

Clear scope, clear price, agreed in writing before anything starts. Fees reflect the work done and the risk taken, nothing more.

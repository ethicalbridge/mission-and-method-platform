What I can help with

The ground I know, mapped to the five foundation steps.

These are the five steps that make up the Method into Impact courses — and the same five that shape any package we scope. Each one I've done hands-on at Save the Children, Lutheran World Federation, AdvocAid, Consulting Base, the Argentine Public Defence or at Ethical Bridge and Method into Impact.

01 · Clarify

Strategic foundations and Theory of Change

  • Organisational health check and baseline
  • Mission, vision, values and positioning
  • Context analysis: PESTLE, stakeholder and actor maps, SWOT
  • Legal-form decision (NGO, association, social enterprise, co-op)
  • Strategic objectives, coded and sequenced
  • Theory of Change pathway: problem → activities → outcomes → impact
02 · Organise

Structure, governance, people and internal systems

  • Org structure, functions map, role cards and org chart
  • Governance pack: delegation matrix, RACI, board calendar, skills matrix
  • Accountability map (upward, horizontal, to communities) and decision log
  • People lifecycle: safer recruitment, contracts, onboarding, check-ins
  • Volunteer policy, agreements, expenses and stipend rules
  • Operating map, SOPs and planning calendar
03 · Prove & Show

MEAL, brand and ethical communications

  • MEAL plan: results framework, indicators, baselines and targets
  • Feedback and accountability routines, learning log, after-action reviews
  • Evaluation ToRs, management response and honest reporting
  • Brand platform: purpose, voice and inclusive language
  • Visual identity, accessibility specs and brand governance
  • Communications plan with ethical storytelling and consent log
04 · Protect

Financial management, legal compliance, policies and risk

  • Annual budget with full cost recovery and scenario planning
  • Cash flow forecast, runway and free-reserves policy
  • Internal controls, approval thresholds and one-page finance manual
  • Core policies: safeguarding/PSEAH, data, anti-fraud, HR, conflicts
  • Legal and compliance map (foreign-funding, AML/CTF, sanctions)
  • Risk register, incident procedures and continuity plans
05 · Sustain & Run

Resource model, partnerships and the annual operating plan

  • Resource model and income mix across funding sources
  • Ethical fundraising and gift-acceptance policy
  • Prospect research, weighted pipeline, case for support
  • Grant management: application → award → cash → spend
  • Partnership strategy, two-way due diligence, MoU design
  • Annual operating plan on a page (KPIs, Gantt, review rhythm)
What I don't do

Clear limits, so expectations are honest.

Scope is as important as scope. These are the lines I don't cross.

  • I'm not part of your team. I prepare, review and coach, but the outputs are owned, held and run by the organisation. The aim is capability, not dependence.
  • I don't promise funding. No donor, platform or grant round can be guaranteed. I prepare you well; the decision stays with the funder.
  • I don't commit your funds, sign contracts or speak publicly in your name.
  • I don't give formal legal opinions. I provide templates and legal-literacy guidance; where you need a formal opinion, work with a lawyer licensed in your jurisdiction.
  • I don't run operations day-to-day. Running the organisation is the team's job; I help build what makes that running possible.
How we work

Practical, collaborative, no dependence.

01 · We agree in writing

Before any paid engagement starts, we sign a one-page working agreement: what I lead, what you decide, what I never do. No surprises later.

02 · I do the work; you own it

I draft, prepare and coordinate so the work doesn't fall on you. Every output has a named owner on your side from week one.

03 · Short cycles, honest feedback

I share drafts early and ask for sign-off within five working days. If something isn't working, I say so the same day and we adjust.

04 · Timelines stay fixed when yours do

I plan every date to be kept. That depends on your team responding on time. If delays start to pile up, we shrink scope rather than move dates.

05 · We leave a handover, not a cliff

Every engagement ends with a named owner on your side for each output, a short guide, and a plan for how your team keeps running it alone.

06 · Fees sustain the platform

Consultation and package fees sustain Method into Impact and let me keep the free library, courses and tools open to organisations that can't yet pay.

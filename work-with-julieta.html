How we start · Free discovery

Three steps before any paid work.

I don't sell hours. I work as a consultancy — before anything is paid, we talk, I understand your situation, and I come back with a proposal shaped around it. If what you need is small enough that one call will cover it, I'll say so.

01

You send a short brief

A 10-minute questionnaire: where you are, what you're trying to build, what's in the way, your indicative timeline and budget. Honest answers only — the brief is just for me.

02

We meet online · 45–60 minutes

A free discovery call. I listen, ask questions, and together we work out whether what you need is a short piece of guidance, one of the packages below, or something else entirely. You leave the call with a clear direction either way.

03

I send a tailored proposal

Within three working days: scope, timeline, indicative budget and a one-page working agreement. The proposal is free and non-binding. If the fit isn't right, I'll say that too and point you somewhere useful.

Path A · Starting from an idea

From an idea to a launch-ready organisation.

Three ways to build the foundations — strategy, Theory of Change, fundraising plan, policies, website and the systems you need to open your doors. Pick the one that matches how much you want to lead the work and how much you want me to do.

A1 · Guided

I guide, you build it.

~6–10 weekly sessions

Weekly coaching while you work through the strategy, Theory of Change, fundraising plan, policies and launch. I review every draft, flag what's missing and keep you unblocked. You own the work from day one.

  • Weekly 1-hour sessions with me
  • Written feedback on every draft between sessions
  • Shared workspace with templates and the course library
  • Honest sign-off at each step before you move on

Good when you have time to do the work and want to learn as you build.

A2 · Most chosen

I build it with you.

~2 months

I build the launch-ready foundation pack alongside you: strategy, Theory of Change, fundraising plan, policies, registration on a key grant platform and a simple website. You end with everything an early organisation needs to open its doors.

  • Strategy and Theory of Change finalised
  • Fundraising priorities, funder shortlist and first application
  • Policy and procedure templates adapted to you
  • Registration on a key grant platform (e.g. GlobalGiving)
  • Simple launch website
  • Hand-over pack with a guide for each output

Good when you need to move fast and want someone experienced beside you.

A3 · Deep

I build, train, and stay for the first cycle.

~4 months

Everything in A2, plus I train you on each output and stay for the first scaling cycle — your first grant application written together, your first partner conversation, your first hire — so you launch with momentum, not just a plan.

  • Everything in A2
  • Training on every tool, document and routine
  • First grant application written together
  • First partner or hire conversation supported
  • 12-month playbook for the next stage

Good when you want to launch strong and have the capacity to grow quickly.

Path B · Small organisation

From a stretched team to order, tools and scale.

For organisations with a small team (typically 2–10) that have grown past their original setup. Three ways to work together, depending on who owns which outputs and how much training the team wants alongside.

B1 · Guided

I coach, your team builds it.

~2–3 months

Fortnightly coaching with you and the leads: diagnose the gaps, agree a priority list, then work through roles, routines, MEAL and policies one at a time. The team does the writing; I review, sign off and keep the pace.

  • Fortnightly 90-minute sessions with the leads
  • Diagnosis of each part of the organisation
  • Written review of every output your team drafts
  • Templates and the course library included

Good when the team has capacity but no clear roadmap.

B2 · Most chosen

I build the systems with the team.

~2–3 months

A short diagnostic, then I build the missing foundations alongside the team: roles with a yearly plan each, MEAL framework and one set of verified figures, donor and partner maps, policies, a grant-ready folder and a first live campaign.

  • Short diagnostic of the whole organisation
  • Roles and a yearly plan per role
  • MEAL framework and one set of verified figures
  • Programme budget, donor map, partner map, risk map
  • Tools set up and loaded with your information
  • Grant-ready folder and a first GlobalGiving campaign
  • Hand-over pack and a guide for each tool

Good when the team is stretched and the gaps are many.

B3 · Deep

I build, train each role, and support scale-up.

~4 months, phased

An Organisational Development Partnership. Deep diagnosis, redesign of roles and routines, each role trained to run its tools, optional on-site missions and a mission playbook for repeating what works. Phased — we agree to continue only if the work is helping.

  • Interviews with team, volunteers, leaders and partners
  • Diagnosis of every part + gap analysis across the whole
  • Roles and routines redesigned with the current team
  • Tools and documents built and loaded with your data
  • Each role trained to run its tools
  • Optional on-site missions (travel costs discussed up front)
  • Mission playbook, learning review and scale-up plan

Good when the organisation is at an inflection point and needs the next-stage setup.

What I don't do

Clear limits, so expectations are honest.

Scope is as important as scope. These are the lines I don't cross.

  • I'm not part of your team. I prepare, review and coach, but the outputs are owned, held and run by the organisation. The aim is capability, not dependence.
  • I don't promise funding. No donor, platform or grant round can be guaranteed. I prepare you well; the decision stays with the funder.
  • I don't commit your funds, sign contracts or speak publicly in your name.
  • I don't give formal legal opinions. I provide templates and legal-literacy guidance; where you need a formal opinion, work with a lawyer licensed in your jurisdiction.
  • I don't run operations day-to-day. Running the organisation is the team's job; I help build what makes that running possible.
How we work

Practical, collaborative, no dependence.

01 · We agree in writing

Before any paid engagement starts, we sign a one-page working agreement: what I lead, what you decide, what I never do. No surprises later.

02 · I do the work; you own it

I draft, prepare and coordinate so the work doesn't fall on you. Every output has a named owner on your side from week one.

03 · Short cycles, honest feedback

I share drafts early and ask for sign-off within five working days. If something isn't working, I say so the same day and we adjust.

04 · Timelines stay fixed when yours do

I plan every date to be kept. That depends on your team responding on time. If delays start to pile up, we shrink scope rather than move dates.

05 · We leave a handover, not a cliff

Every engagement ends with a named owner on your side for each output, a short guide, and a plan for how your team keeps running it alone.

06 · Fees sustain the platform

Consultation and package fees sustain Method into Impact and let me keep the free library, courses and tools open to organisations that can't yet pay.

Path A · Starting from an idea

From an idea to a launch-ready organisation.

Path B · Small organisation

From a stretched team to order, tools and scale.

For organisations with a small team (typically 2–10) that have grown past their original setup. Three ways to work together, depending on who owns which outputs and how much training the team wants alongside.

B1 · Guided

I coach, your team builds it.

~2–3 months

Fortnightly coaching with you and the leads: diagnose the gaps, agree a priority list, then work through roles, routines, MEAL and policies one at a time. The team does the writing; I review, sign off and keep the pace.

  • Diagnosis of each part of the organisation
  • Written review of every output your team drafts
  • Templates and the course library included

Good when the team has capacity but no clear roadmap.

I build the systems with the team.

~2–3 months

A short diagnostic, then I build the missing foundations alongside the team: roles with a yearly plan each, MEAL framework and one set of verified figures, donor and partner maps, policies, a grant-ready folder and a first live campaign.

What I don't do

Clear limits, so expectations are honest.

Scope is as important as scope. These are the lines I don't cross.

  • I'm not part of your team. I prepare, review and coach, but the outputs are owned, held and run by the organisation. The aim is capability, not dependence.
  • I don't promise funding. No donor, platform or grant round can be guaranteed. I prepare you well; the decision stays with the funder.
  • I don't commit your funds, sign contracts or speak publicly in your name.
  • I don't give formal legal opinions. I provide templates and legal-literacy guidance; where you need a formal opinion, work with a lawyer licensed in your jurisdiction.
  • I don't run operations day-to-day. Running the organisation is the team's job; I help build what makes that running possible.
How we work

Practical, collaborative, no dependence.

01 · We agree in writing

Before any paid engagement starts, we sign a one-page working agreement: what I lead, what you decide, what I never do. No surprises later.

02 · I do the work; you own it

I draft, prepare and coordinate so the work doesn't fall on you. Every output has a named owner on your side from week one.

03 · Short cycles, honest feedback

I share drafts early and ask for sign-off within five working days. If something isn't working, I say so the same day and we adjust.

04 · Timelines stay fixed when yours do

I plan every date to be kept. That depends on your team responding on time. If delays start to pile up, we shrink scope rather than move dates.

05 · We leave a handover, not a cliff

Every engagement ends with a named owner on your side for each output, a short guide, and a plan for how your team keeps running it alone.

06 · Fees sustain the platform

Consultation and package fees sustain Method into Impact and let me keep the free library, courses and tools open to organisations that can't yet pay.

Module 01Strategic Foundations8 lessons+

Make the connected decisions everything you build will depend on: why your initiative should exist, the context it will enter, who matters, the future you want, the work you will do, how you will behave, what kind of organisation to create, where you can add unique value and what you will prioritise first. Plan them for one to five years ahead. If your idea is very new, plan for a shorter time, or plan for five years with a one-year pilot and review everything after that first year. This foundation will guide all your work, so come back to it and review it often.

Module 02Theory of Change9 lessons+

In this module you explain how the work you are planning could lead to the change you want to see. You first look at a whole Theory of Change, then build your own one step at a time, the way the work will happen: the problem, the inputs, the activities, the outputs, the outcomes and the long-term impact. Finally, you test the assumptions that connect the steps and read the whole pathway back to check every link. Before you launch, it is a reasoned hypothesis to test with the people affected, not proof that your idea works.

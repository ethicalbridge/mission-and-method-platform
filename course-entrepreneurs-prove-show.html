Module 06MEAL: Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning12 lessons+

Build evidence into your idea from the start. Turn your Theory of Change into a results framework, define indicators with honest baselines and targets, plan how you will collect data ethically, listen to the first people you serve, decide what you will evaluate and use what you learn to shape the next step. The same principles apply whether you are building a nonprofit, a social business, a cooperative or a community initiative, and whether you call it MEAL or impact measurement.

Module 08Marketing & Communications14 lessons+

Plan how you will communicate what you are building and reach the people your idea needs from the start: the people you want to serve, communities, first customers or members, supporters, funders, partners, volunteers and the public. Build a communications strategy on your brand and your early evidence, choose channels you can sustain, tell stories ethically from the very first post and measure what actually helps.

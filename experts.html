Every organisation is different. The scope, timeline and price will depend on your starting point, priorities and the support you need.

How we work
Our support is practical, collaborative and adapted to your context. We work with organisations to create useful foundations that they can understand, own and continue using independently.

We do not believe in one-size-fits-all solutions.

Consultation fees and tailored-package prices help us sustain the Mission & Method platform and continue making practical learning tools more accessible to organisations worldwide.

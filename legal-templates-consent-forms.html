Consent forms

Consent forms help people understand a specific activity or use of their information and record a clear choice where consent is appropriate.

Use these outlines to explain the choice in plain language and record it. A legal reviewer should confirm when consent is the right basis and what local rules require.

Templates only — legal review required

These downloadable outlines are general starting points, not legal advice or ready-to-use documents. Before using, signing, publishing or relying on any template, have a qualified legal professional review and adapt it to your organisation, the relevant jurisdiction, local laws and regulations, sector rules and current practice. Check any mandatory wording, consent requirements, safeguarding duties, privacy rules and limits on liability.

Mission & Method does not guarantee that an outline is complete, current, suitable or legally enforceable for your circumstances. You are responsible for obtaining advice and approving the final document. Any limitation of responsibility is subject to applicable law and does not remove rights or duties that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Founder story

I built the kind of path I once needed.

I am Julieta Castineira de Dios, founder of Ethical Bridge and Builder System. I know how it feels when a meaningful vision is bigger than the structure around it.

Julieta Castineira de DiosFounder of Ethical Bridge & Builder System
My experience

Building across borders, from purpose to practice.

My work has taken me through international consulting, project administration, awards and compliance, development management, research and legal work. I have contributed to organisations including Save the Children, Lutheran World Federation, Consulting Base and AdvocAid across Australia, Europe and Africa.

I bring legal training, a Master in African Studies, fluent English, Spanish and Portuguese, and years of experience working with people, partners, systems and complex priorities. I also bring a practical instinct shaped by growing up on a farm in Argentina: start, learn, make, repair and keep going.

“Ethical Bridge began with a belief: people and grassroots organisations should be able to find each other, work together and create real impact across borders.”

Why Builder System exists

This platform is the result of creating Ethical Bridge from scratch.

Building Ethical Bridge showed me that ambitious ideas do not become real through motivation alone. They become real when you learn how to make decisions, create a strategy, design an organisation, manage uncertainty and keep moving when the path is not yet clear.

Builder System shares that lived process with early-stage founders. It is not a collection of abstract lessons. Every module helps you create the practical work an organisation needs to become real, useful and sustainable.

About Mission & Method

Learning, tools and people to help you build what matters.

Mission & Method helps purpose-led founders and organisations move from a meaningful idea to clear strategy, workable systems and confident action. Courses, evidence, templates, digital tools and specialist support come together in one place.

Explore the platform
Our method
  1. Learn the decisions behind a strong organisation.
  2. Build practical assets using exercises and tools.
  3. Connect with relevant people and support.
Why we exist

Purpose needs a method.

Founders often have a clear reason to act but limited time, guidance and infrastructure to turn that purpose into a sustainable organisation. Mission & Method makes the steps visible and gives you materials to adapt to your own context.

The founder

Built from the work of building.

Julieta Castiñeira de Dios

Julieta is a lawyer, strategist and organisation builder, and the founder of Ethical Bridge and Local Impact Alliance. Taking Ethical Bridge from an idea to an organisation ready to launch shaped the frameworks, decisions and tools behind Mission & Method.

Her experience across legal practice, international development, research, programmes and partnerships informs a method designed for founders who need clarity they can put to work.

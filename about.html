WHY WE EXIST

Four gaps between purpose and practice.

Method into Impact is designed to close all four together.

01

Structure

Founders know why they exist but not what to decide first. Strategy lives in scattered notes, and good ideas stall before they can recruit, fundraise or partner.

02

Knowledge

Research, standards and an organisation's own learning are scattered. Decisions rest on guesswork, and impact is hard to show.

03

Systems

Strategy, projects, people, funding and reporting sit in spreadsheets and apps that do not connect. Plans rarely become daily practice.

04

Support

Specialist advice is costly and hard to find for small teams, so the hardest decisions are often made alone.

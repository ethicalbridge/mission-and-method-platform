Planning System · Organisation Systems

Make the organisation behind the idea strong.

These seven sub-modules follow your Internal Strategic Objectives folders. Each one creates a system your organisation can use immediately.

A1

Image & Branding

Brand identity, messaging, tone of voice, visual direction and brand-consistency checks.

A2

Marketing & Communication

Audience, content calendar, campaigns, stakeholder communication and performance dashboard.

A3

MEAL

Monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning: indicators, feedback, data collection and impact reporting.

A4

Organisational Activity Coordination

Operating calendar, RACI, workflows, meeting rhythm, decisions and weekly coordination.

A5

Organisational Leadership Strategy

Organisation chart, roles, governance, values, team development, inclusion and wellbeing.

A6

Financial & Legal Procedures

Budgets, cash flow, controls, procurement, compliance, risk and delegation of authority.

A7

Data Protection System

Data inventory, privacy assessment, consent, retention, access control and incident response.

Module 2 · Planning System

Turn strategic objectives into a plan your team can deliver.

Build detailed activities first. Then use their owners, dates, status and dependencies to create the timeline and Gantt chart.

1. Strategic Activity Plan

Break each ESO and ISO into outputs, activities, owners, dates, KPIs, risks and completion evidence.

2. Timeline & Gantt

Generate a yearly and quarterly schedule directly from the activity plan—never from guesswork.

3. Team Work Plan

Give every person a filtered, clear view of their responsibilities, priorities and deadlines.

Team & operating structure

Give the plan the people and routines it needs.

Organigram

Map governance, paid roles, external advisors, reporting lines and hiring phases.

Role design

Define purpose, responsibilities, decision rights and the systems each role owns.

Onboarding

Use a 30-day checklist so every staff member, intern or volunteer learns the strategic plan, policies and working systems.

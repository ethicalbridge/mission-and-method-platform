Cookies & browser storage

What are cookies, and why we use them

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to store, so it can remember things between pages or visits — your language choice, whether you have already dismissed a message, or (for analytics) that your two visits came from the same browser. "Browser storage" (localStorage, sessionStorage) is a similar mechanism without the automatic server round-trip; this site uses both.

On this site, cookies and browser storage are used for two purposes only, and nothing else:

  1. To make the site work — remember your language, remember that you have answered the analytics banner, keep the mobile menu stable between pages, and (on the preview pages) remember that you have entered the preview passcode so you do not have to re-enter it every time.
  2. To understand what helps whom — if you accept the banner, Google Analytics writes two cookies in your browser so repeat visits can be counted as one visitor rather than two, and so pages can be grouped into sessions. Nothing is shared with advertisers. Nothing identifies you by name.

The full list of what this site stores on your device

Strictly functional — always set

These are needed for the site to work. Under ePrivacy they do not require consent.

  • mim-lang (localStorage, no expiry): your language choice (English or Spanish). Written by the EN/ES toggle.
  • mim-consent-v1 (localStorage, no expiry): your choice on the analytics banner (accepted or declined). Without this, the banner would reappear on every page.
  • mip_gate_v2 (sessionStorage, cleared when you close the browser): remembers you have entered the preview passcode so you do not re-enter it on every page in the same session.
  • Mobile-menu open/closed state (localStorage, no expiry): so the menu does not flicker as you move between pages.

Analytics — only set if you clicked Accept

These are the only non-functional cookies. If you clicked Decline (or have not yet answered the banner), neither is written.

  • _ga (first-party cookie set by Google Analytics, 2-year expiry): a random pseudonymous ID that lets Analytics tell one browser from another, so your 5 pageviews are counted as one visitor rather than five.
  • _ga_2VLHVZSVTD (first-party cookie set by Google Analytics, 2-year expiry): persists session state (when a visit started, what pages it covered) for the Method into Impact Analytics property.

No advertising cookies. No cross-site trackers. No social-media pixels. No fingerprinting. Nothing is set by any third party except the two Google Analytics cookies above, and only if you opted in.

How to see and delete cookies in your browser

You can inspect and remove any cookie, including the ones this site uses, from your browser's own settings. Short guides:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data → See all site data and permissions → search "methodintoimpact.com" → delete.
  • Safari: Safari → Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data → search "methodintoimpact.com" → Remove.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data → search "methodintoimpact.com" → Remove Selected.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data → See all cookies and site data → search "methodintoimpact.com" → delete.

You can also tell your browser to block all cookies, or just third-party cookies, in the same settings. This site functions with cookies blocked, but your language choice and consent decision will not be remembered between visits.

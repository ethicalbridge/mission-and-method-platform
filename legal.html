Who runs this site

Method into Impact is a one-practitioner consultancy operated by Julieta Castiñeira de Dios, based in Indonesia (UTC+7).

For any question about this site, the data it holds, or anything legal, write to julieta@methodintoimpact.com. For general questions, hello@methodintoimpact.com is the shared inbox.

Privacy

What personal data this site holds

The only personal data this site handles is what you choose to send through it:

  • The discovery-brief form on the Consult page (name, email, organisation, country, website, stage, timeline, which option interests you, main challenge, indicative budget, best times for a call).
  • Any email you send to hello@methodintoimpact.com or julieta@methodintoimpact.com.

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies. No visitor profile is built.

Lawful basis (GDPR, for EU/EEA/UK visitors)

The discovery brief is handled under Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR: processing necessary for steps you have requested before entering into a contract. If you later agree to be added to a mailing list for a specific launch (for example the courses opening), that is handled separately under Article 6(1)(a), consent, which you can withdraw at any time by replying to any email I send.

Retention

If a discovery brief leads to a paid engagement, your data is kept for the duration of the engagement and up to six years after it ends, for tax and legal records. If no engagement starts, your brief is deleted twelve months after our last correspondence. Launch waitlist emails are kept until the launch they relate to is complete, then deleted unless you ask to stay on a list for the next one.

Your rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR and comparable laws elsewhere, you can ask me to: give you a copy of what I hold on you (access), correct it (rectification), delete it (erasure), restrict how it is used, port it to another service, or object to processing. Write to julieta@methodintoimpact.com. I respond within 30 days. If you believe your rights have been breached, you can complain to your national data protection authority.

Sub-processors

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. (United States). Email reaches me through standard commercial email infrastructure, which may transit through GitHub, Google, Microsoft or Cloudflare servers depending on the sender. The discovery-brief form is currently in prototype mode and does not yet transmit submissions; once wired for live use, it will send through a dedicated form service (candidates: Formspree, Netlify Forms, Basin) and this page will be updated to name the provider.

Cross-border transfers

Because the site is hosted in the United States (GitHub Pages) and I operate from Indonesia, your personal data crosses borders when you send me a brief or an email. For EU/EEA/UK visitors, these transfers rely on either (a) the submission being necessary to perform the service you have asked for, or (b) your explicit act of sending the data knowing it will be processed internationally. No onward transfer is made beyond what is required to deliver and read the message.

Please do not send bank details, government ID numbers, passwords, health records or other sensitive information through the discovery form or by email. For sensitive materials once an engagement begins, we will agree a secure channel in the Terms of Reference.

Storage & cookies

This site stores a few small things in your browser so it works as expected. All functional, none for tracking:

  • mim-lang (localStorage): remembers your language choice (English or Spanish).
  • mip_gate_v2 (sessionStorage): remembers that you have entered the preview passcode, so you do not re-enter it on every page in the same browser session. Cleared when you close the browser.
  • The mobile menu's open/closed state (localStorage): so the menu does not flicker as you move between pages.

No analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, no third-party trackers. Because all stored items are strictly functional, no cookie-consent banner is required under ePrivacy or GDPR. You can clear any of this at any time in your browser settings.

Terms of use

The site is provided as-is for anyone who wants to read it or get in touch. Reading the site and sending a discovery brief do not, on their own, create a contractual relationship. A paid engagement starts only after we both sign a short Terms of Reference agreed between us.

The discovery call and the tailored proposal that follow are free and non-binding. If a proposal is accepted, the Terms of Reference signed between us becomes the governing agreement for that engagement, and sets fees, scope, deliverables, timeline, confidentiality and the rest.

You agree not to use this site to send abusive messages, submit false information in the discovery form, attempt to breach the preview passcode gate, or scrape the site in ways that affect its availability for others.

About legal and professional advice

Nothing provided on this site, in a discovery call, or in a tailored proposal is legal advice. Even where I draw on my legal background, no lawyer–client relationship is formed by using this site or by engaging my consultancy. For jurisdiction-specific legal matters, engage counsel licensed where you operate.

The same applies to tax, financial, safeguarding and clinical matters: this consultancy produces general strategy, systems and process guidance, not specialist advice regulated in your country. For those matters, work with a qualified professional in your jurisdiction.

Accessibility

I aim for WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard navigation, labelled form fields, visible focus states, responsive mobile layouts, and respect for the reduced-motion system preference. Testing is ongoing and the site is a work in progress. If something is hard to use, write to hello@methodintoimpact.com and I will address it.

Security

The site is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. There is no login, no payment flow and no sensitive data stored server-side here. Transport is over HTTPS. The preview passcode gate on the courses and resources pages is a soft "under construction" measure for a prototype — it is not a security boundary and should not be treated as one.

Please do not send bank details, passwords, government IDs or health records through the discovery form or by email. For sensitive materials once an engagement starts, we will agree a secure channel in the Terms of Reference.

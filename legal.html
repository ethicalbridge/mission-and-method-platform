About this development preview.

Privacy

Course answers and software demo records are stored in this browser. They are not sent to an account service. Contact drafts download to your device and are not submitted. Hosting providers receive ordinary page requests; styles may request fonts from Google Fonts.

Do not enter confidential participant, personnel or financial records in this preview. Export your work before clearing browser storage. The operating entity, support contact, retention rules and production privacy terms must be confirmed before launch.

Preview use

This is an unfinished development preview for review and learning. No paid account, course purchase or software subscription can be created here. Commercial terms will be provided before checkout is enabled.

Storage and cookies

This preview uses local browser storage for requested draft-saving features. It does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Automatic translation and optional analytics are not enabled. You can manage this site’s stored data in your browser settings after exporting a backup.

Security

The static preview has no authenticated storage or paid-access enforcement. Browser drafts can be accessed by someone using the same browser profile. Backups contain your entered answers. Production access, private downloads and cloud saving require a server-side service before commercial launch.

Accessibility

The learning workspace supports keyboard navigation, labelled fields, visible focus, mobile layouts and reduced motion. Course diagrams also provide text descriptions. Accessibility testing is ongoing; the contact page can prepare a downloadable feedback draft.

Educational guidance

The course supports general organisational planning. It does not determine the legal, tax, financial or safeguarding requirements that apply to your situation. Check relevant local requirements and obtain qualified advice when needed.

Sources and copyright

New lesson explanations and examples are original educational content. Fictional examples are labelled. Further-reading links point to the relevant publisher. Third-party materials remain subject to their owners’ terms.

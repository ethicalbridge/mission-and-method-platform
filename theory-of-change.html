Module 3 · Worked example

Theory of Change & organisation structure.

Ethical Bridge / SRL shows how the mission, change pathway and early roles can reinforce each other.

Ethical Bridge mission

Connect purpose-driven individuals with grassroots organisations worldwide, enabling meaningful work, collaboration and positive impact.

The change pathway

Follow the logic from problem to impact.

Problem

Ethical organisations lack visibility and global connections; meaningful opportunities are fragmented.

Inputs

Digital expertise, staff, funding, partnerships, IT infrastructure, verification standards and outreach.

Outputs

Verified digital hub, organisation profiles, ethical opportunities, campaigns and transparent data.

Outcomes

Organisations gain recognition and partnerships; more people access ethical work and contribute to impact.

Impact

A connected, inclusive world where ethical collaboration drives social, environmental and economic change.

Your Theory of Change is not a diagram alone. It is the logic that tells you what to build, what to measure and what must be true for your mission to succeed.

Initial organisation structure

Assign clear ownership to the work.

Chief Executive Officer

Strategy, mission, partnerships, fundraising, governance, risk and organisational performance.

Finance Manager

Budgets, reporting, compliance, grants and financial risk.

Communications & Marketing

Brand, stories, campaigns, community engagement and analytics.

Web / Software

Digital platform development, maintenance and user experience.

Business Development

Ethical partnerships, donors, sponsors and long-term revenue.

Customer Service

User support, platform quality and feedback-led improvement.

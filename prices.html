WHICH COUNTRY COUNTS

Your price depends on the country where the card or account you pay with was issued. If you live in one country but pay with a card from another, the card's country sets the price. Checkout shows your band and the reason before you pay.

PRICE QUESTIONS

Fair, honest, no surprises.

How do the country price bands work?

Prices follow the World Bank's country income groups. Upper-middle income countries pay 60% of the standard price, lower-middle 30% and low income 15%, with a minimum of $9. Your band comes from the country where the card you pay with was issued, and checkout shows which band applies and why. See which band your country is in.

What is the launch price?

A lower price for the first buyers while we open. It applies until 31 March 2027 or the first 200 buyers, whichever comes first. After that the standard price shown on each card applies.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days for a course if you have opened fewer than 20% of its lessons, and within 7 days for a pack or library if none of its files has been downloaded. A single template cannot be refunded once downloaded, unless the file is faulty. Read the refund policy.

How long can I use a course?

For as long as Method into Impact offers the course, and at least five years from your purchase. Your Packs are always downloadable, so your work stays yours.

Are the templates legal advice?

No. Each template is a general starting point for small purpose-led organisations and entrepreneurs in different countries. Adapt it to your organisation and have a qualified adviser check it against the law where you operate before you adopt, sign or publish it.

Can I share the templates with other organisations?

Templates are licensed for use within one organisation. If you are a consultant and want to use them with clients, write to us. Reselling or republishing the blank templates is not allowed.

Can I get a refund?

Refund terms will be published before checkout opens. In the current preview, no payments are processed.

What if I can't pay?

We offer a number of free places every quarter. Write to us and tell us what you are building.

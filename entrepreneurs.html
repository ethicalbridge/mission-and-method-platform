The method, at your stage

Five steps from an idea to a first plan.

The same five steps organisations use, kept simple and in the order a new idea needs them.

  1. 1Clarify your idea: the problem, who has it, and talking to people before you build.
  2. 2Shape a first version: your solution, a small pilot, your team and the form you need.
  3. 3Show it can work: simple measures, why you, and a one-minute pitch.
  4. 4Start safely: your first budget, people and data kept safe, and the risks that could stop you.
  5. 5Fund it and plan 90 days: your first money and the next three months.

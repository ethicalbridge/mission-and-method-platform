ABOUT THIS COURSE

Replace scattered ideas with a plan you can use.

This is the initial planning pathway for early-stage founders, social entrepreneurs, NGOs, community projects and purpose-led organisation builders. You do not need a finished business plan, a team or previous strategy experience.

Work through the connected questions every organisation must answer: Why do we exist? What change are we creating? Who does what? How will we operate safely? What must happen next? How will the model sustain itself?

This course is right for you if…

  • You have a meaningful idea but need a clear sequence.
  • Your strategy and systems are fragmented.
  • You are preparing to recruit, fundraise, partner or launch.
  • You want practical assets as well as an understanding of the method.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Questions, answered.

What is included in the course price?

The course includes lessons, examples, exercises, downloadable resources and associated Excel tools. See the Pricing & packages page for the launch price and package comparison.

Do I need to subscribe to software?

No. The Software Suite is an optional subscription. Each tool works independently. The course alone does not grant a Suite subscription; the optional launch bundle explicitly includes both.

How is my progress saved?

Drafts and completion are stored in this browser. Use Your Organisation to export a backup and import it on another device. This preview does not sync to an account.

Can I download the Excel resources now?

The founder's files are awaiting upload. Their course location and access requirements are prepared in the resource library. The on-page exercises and strategy-pack export work now.

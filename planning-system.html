Start here

You don’t need a finished plan.

This course is for first-time founders, community organisations, NGOs and purpose-led businesses. Begin with the idea you have and identify what still needs testing.

Each lesson follows the same sequence: learn, see an example, reflect, complete your exercise and save. Your answers feed the module output and remain available in later course lessons.

What to expect

  • 8 modules · 45 guided lessons and reviews
  • Approximately 14–16 hours, including exercises
  • Work at your own pace and revise earlier answers
  • Eight outputs in Your Organisation
  • No software subscription needed to complete the course

This preview uses written lessons. Instructor videos and the founder’s Excel files have not yet been supplied.

Learning outcomes

Know what you’re building.

By the end of the course, you will be able to:

  • Explain your purpose, vision, mission, values and strategic priorities.
  • Describe how your work could contribute to change and test the assumptions behind it.
  • Assign roles, organise internal systems and prioritise policies.
  • Turn priorities into a work plan, delivery model and funding approach.
Skills & tools

Practise each skill as you learn.

Build skills in strategic planning, impact logic, organisation design, coordination and funding. Guided exercises turn those skills into eight reviewable outputs.

Your course includes its associated Excel resources, which are awaiting upload in this preview. Separate software subscriptions are optional.

Before you begin

What is included in the course price?

The course includes lessons, examples, exercises, downloadable resources and associated Excel tools. The price is not confirmed yet. Course checkout is not active in this preview.

Do I need to subscribe to software?

No. Software products are optional, separate subscriptions. Each works independently. Purchasing a course will not grant software access, and a software subscription will not grant course access.

How is my progress saved?

Drafts and completion are stored in this browser. Use Your Organisation to export a backup and import it on another device. This preview does not sync to an account.

The founder’s files are awaiting upload. Their course location and access requirements are prepared in the resource library. The on-page exercises and strategy-pack export work now.

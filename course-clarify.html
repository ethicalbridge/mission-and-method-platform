Module 02Theory of Change10 lessons · 15 resources+

Explain how your work could contribute to change. Work backwards from the future you seek, then check the pathway forwards. Build one pathway for each external objective. Your map is a reasoned hypothesis to test with the people affected, not proof that an intervention works.

Purpose, Strategy & Theory of Change

About Course 1

Why should this exist, and what change do you want to make?

Make the connected decisions everything else depends on: the need, your purpose, the people who matter, vision, mission, values, the form you will take, your place among others and your first objectives. Then build a reasoned pathway from the problem to long-term change.

You'll learn to

  • Test the need and write a purpose, vision and mission that hold up.
  • Map your context, the people involved and what makes you different.
  • Build a Theory of Change before you build the activities.

Who will decide, who will do the work, and how will it run?

COURSE QUESTIONS

What to know about the courses.

I only have an idea. Is this for me?

Yes. The courses assume nothing is built yet: no team, no board, no budget and no registration. Each lesson helps you decide what to set up now and what can wait until you grow.

How is this different from the courses for organisations?

Same method and the same modules, written for someone building something rather than running it. The organisation track adds a fifth course, Sustain & Run, for organisations that are already operating.

Do I have to take the courses in order?

They are designed in sequence: Course 1 feeds Course 2, and so on. You can jump ahead, but each later course uses the decisions you made in the ones before it.

How long will it take?

Self-paced. Each course takes about 7 to 11 hours including the exercises: Clarify about 7, Organise about 10, Prove & Show about 11, Protect about 8. The free Module 0 takes about an hour: four short lessons of 15 to 20 minutes each.

Can I get help while I build?

Yes. Julieta works one-to-one with entrepreneurs on the same method: she can build the documents for you or build them with you.

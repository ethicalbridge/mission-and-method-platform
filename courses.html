COURSE QUESTIONS

What to know about the courses.

Do I have to take the courses in order?

The courses are designed in sequence — Course 1 feeds Course 2, which feeds Course 3, and so on. You can jump ahead, but each later course assumes the outputs of the ones before it. Most people get more value working through them in order.

Can I buy just one course?

Yes. Each course is a separate one-time purchase with lifetime access and all its Excel workbooks included. The Full Pathway bundle gives you all five at a measurable saving.

What does "You build" mean?

Each course ends with a Pack — a set of practical outputs for your organisation (strategy, Theory of Change, People Plan, MEAL framework, and so on). Together the five Packs form your organisation pack, ready to share with your board, team or funders.

How long will each course take?

Self-paced. The estimates (~8 to ~15 hours per course) assume you also do the guided exercises. The full pathway is around 52 hours of focused work spread over as long as you need.

Can I preview a course before buying?

Yes. Module 0 (Start Here) is free. Open the first lesson, describe your organisation and run a baseline health check — no card required.

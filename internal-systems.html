Internal Systems & Operations

See how the internal systems of a strong organisation fit together, and choose the minimum set your stage needs. This module is a map: it introduces each system and the decisions it needs, and points to the later modules that go deeper into people, MEAL, finance, legal compliance and risk, and planning.

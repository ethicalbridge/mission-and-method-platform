Use it for standard and enhanced checks under your Partner Due Diligence and Sanctions Screening Policy. For light checks, Part A section 1, the sanctions declaration and the documents checklist are usually enough: delete the rest. Translate it into the language your partner works in, and offer to complete it together by phone or visit. If the partner has had a recent assessment by another reputable funder, ask for that first and only send the questions it does not answer. Delete these orange boxes before sending.
:::

# About this questionnaire

[[Organisation name]] would like to work with your organisation. Before we agree a partnership or a grant, we ask every partner the same questions. They help us understand how your organisation works, agree the right level of support and meet the requirements of our funders and the law.

There are no trick questions. Many small organisations do not yet have every policy or system listed here, and that is fine. A "no" or "not yet" will not, by itself, stop a partnership. It helps us agree where we can support you. Please answer honestly: an incorrect answer causes far more difficulty than a gap.

- **Who completes it:** your executive head or a person they authorise.
- **How:** type or write your answers in the spaces, or answer by phone or in a meeting with [[Name, role]].
- **When:** please return it by [[date]], at least [[10 working days]] after you receive it. Ask us if you need more time.
- **Where to send it:** [[compliance@yourorganisation.org]] or [[postal address / secure upload link]].
- **Questions:** contact [[Name, role]] at [[email / phone]].

**How we use your information.** We use your answers only to assess and manage our partnership, and we may share them with our funders or auditors where our agreements require it. We keep them securely for [[7 years]] after the partnership ends and then delete them. Our Data Protection Policy explains your rights, including how to see or correct the information we hold. [[Add any notice required by your local data protection law.]]

::: adapt Privacy notice
The questionnaire collects personal data about the partner's board members and staff, including names and possibly dates of birth for sanctions screening. Keep the request to what you need. Check your local data protection law, for example the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 or South Africa's POPIA, for any information you must give people when you collect their data. Ask for dates of birth only for people you will screen, and only if a possible match would otherwise be hard to rule out.
:::

::: adapt Registration in your context
In some countries, non-profits must register with a specific regulator and renew each year, and receiving foreign funds may need separate approval. Add questions that matter in the countries where your partners work, and check your local law on what registration a partner needs for the activity you will fund. ::: adapt Safeguarding questions
Keep the last question sensitive. The aim is to see whether the partner recognises and responds to concerns, not to obtain confidential case details. A partner that reports "no concerns ever" may simply lack reporting routes, so ask how people would raise one. If the partner works with children, you may add questions based on the Keeping Children Safe standards. Check your local law on which criminal record checks are available and lawful, as these vary widely between countries.
:::

# Guidance notes for staff

- **Explain first.** Before sending the questionnaire, call the partner to explain why we ask and offer help.
- **Read for meaning, not form.** A clear, honest description of how a small group manages cash can be stronger than a long policy copied from elsewhere.
- **Follow up gaps kindly.** Ask how things work in practice before rating an area.
- **Check what matters most for this partnership.** A partner that will never meet children needs a lighter safeguarding check than one running a school programme.
- **Record your reasons.** Funders and auditors look for the reasons behind a rating, not only the rating.
- **Keep it confidential.** Store the completed questionnaire with restricted access, as set out in our Partner Due Diligence and Sanctions Screening Policy.

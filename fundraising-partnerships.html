Module 5 · Planning System

Fundraising, sponsors & strategic partnerships.

Build a revenue system that fits your mission, capacity and stage—then turn the right opportunities into lasting relationships.

What you will build

A focused, ethical funding pipeline.

This module uses a real organisational case study to make fundraising practical, strategic and sustainable.

1. Revenue strategy

Choose the income sources that suit your business or organisation now, and identify what should wait.

2. Go / No-Go map

Assess funding opportunities for values fit, likelihood, readiness, capacity and risk before pursuing them.

3. Engagement plan

Track the relationship, the next useful action and the year-round plan for priority supporters.

Worked example · Ethical Bridge / SRL

A phased model for sustainable income.

Foundation year

Launch the directory, build trust and validate demand through individual giving, memberships and aligned corporate partnerships.

Grow the system

Strengthen relationships, capacity and reporting before introducing more complex grant and institutional funding.

Scale product revenue

Once demand is proven, make the jobs portal a simple, mission-aligned revenue engine.

Practical tools

Use these in sequence.

Tool A

Revenue Strategy Canvas

Set income targets, choose priority streams and align your funding plan with real capacity.

Tool B

Go / No-Go Opportunity Assessment

Screen donors and sponsors before investing time in a relationship or proposal.

Tool C

Supporter CRM & Engagement Planner

Record history, contacts, relationship health, actions and quarterly engagement.

Tool D

Fundraising Pipeline Dashboard

Manage opportunities from research to renewal with clear ownership and next actions.

Fund the work without losing the mission.

Every opportunity should create more capacity for meaningful impact—not compromise the values that make the work matter.

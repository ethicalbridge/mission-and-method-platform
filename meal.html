Course 3 · Prove & Show · Module 06

MEAL: Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning

Turn your Theory of Change into evidence you can use. Build a results framework, define indicators with honest baselines and targets, collect data ethically, listen to the people you serve, evaluate what matters and use what you learn to make better decisions. The same principles apply to programme MEAL in nonprofits and to impact measurement in social enterprises and startups.

3 modules, each opening to its lessons.

Module 03Structure, Governance & Accountability10 lessons+

Move from your mission and strategy to who will actually do what. Design the work before the job titles, decide early how governance and management will be kept apart, agree who can decide what, and make accountability and coordination visible from day one. A founder working alone, or two co-founders, need this clarity as much as a team of two hundred, and it is far easier to set up now than to untangle later.

Module 04People, Culture & Volunteers10 lessons+

Structure says who does what; this module helps you find, support and keep the people who will do it. Plan the mix of co-founders, first hires, contractors and volunteers, recruit fairly from your very first role, start people well, help them grow, look after wellbeing from day one and make sure that what people know does not leave with them.

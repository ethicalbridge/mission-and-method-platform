Price by country band

Country is set by billing address. Organisations pay the higher of their country of registration and their head office. Countries on the World Bank's FCV List move to the low-income band.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Pricing questions, answered.

Are these prices final?

They're working assumptions to test with real buyers. Launch prices are set; some regular prices are proposals we'll confirm after the first cohort. If a price changes after you buy, you keep the price you paid.

Do I have to buy the software to use the course?

No. The course works fully on its own — all Excel resources are included in the course price. The Software Suite is a separate, optional subscription.

Do I have to buy the course to use the software?

No. Each offer stands alone. Established organisations often start with Tools or Support without taking a course.

How is my country's price decided?

By billing address, using the World Bank's income group. Organisations pay the higher of their country of registration and their head office band. Countries on the World Bank's FCV List move to the low-income band.

Who is the "build it for you" option for?

Founders and small teams who need the strategic foundation done to a high standard on a tight timeline, or organisations with capacity but no time to run the course themselves. Send a short brief and we'll come back with a proposal.

When does checkout open?

Once the accounts and payments review passes (the "Launch readiness" gate of our strategic roadmap). Until then, you can preview lessons and save your work in this browser.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Pricing questions, answered.

Are these prices final?

They're working assumptions to test with real buyers. Launch prices for the Planning System, Legal Templates and Our Support are set. Software Suite paid plans are proposed prices we'll confirm before checkout opens. If a price changes after you buy, you keep the price you paid.

What's actually available today?

The Planning System course (five courses; Module 0 is free; previews now, full sale once the Excel workbooks upload), Evidence Library (free, always), Legal Templates ($9 each or $49 for all), Our Support ($49/hr consultations and packages), and free browser tools from the Software Suite. Paid Software Suite plans are on the roadmap.

Is the Evidence Library really free?

Yes — the Evidence Library is free for everyone, everywhere, with no account required. It's part of how we help organisations make evidence-informed decisions.

What do I get for $9 (or $49) on Legal Templates?

$9 buys a single template — a policy, procedure, contract or consent form written as a starting outline to adapt with qualified local legal review. $49 unlocks every template in the library. Templates are not legal advice, and you always need someone qualified in your jurisdiction to review before you use them.

Do I have to buy the software to use the course?

No. The course works fully on its own — all Excel resources are included in the course price. The Software Suite is a separate, optional subscription that isn't launched yet.

How is my country's price decided?

By billing address, using the World Bank's income group. Organisations pay the higher of their country of registration and their head office band. Countries on the World Bank's FCV List move to the low-income band.

Who is the "build it for you" option for?

Founders and small teams who need the strategic foundation done to a high standard on a tight timeline, or organisations with capacity but no time to run the course themselves. Send a short brief and we'll come back with a proposal.

When does checkout open?

Once the accounts and payments review passes (the "Launch readiness" gate of our strategic roadmap). Until then, you can preview lessons, browse the Evidence Library and save your work in this browser.

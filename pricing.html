PRICING QUESTIONS

Fair, honest, no surprises.

Why is the launch price so low?

These are launch prices for the development preview. We publish them openly so the first group of founders can afford to try the method. Regular prices will apply once the full lesson videos and checkout are live.

Are there country bands?

Yes. We apply fair-price country bands at checkout so the course is affordable regardless of where you build your organisation. Published prices above are the high-income band.

What's the difference between the course and the Impact Tools?

The course teaches the method and gives you Excel workbooks you keep forever. The Impact Tools subscription brings every output into a single connected workspace with cloud saving and reporting. Both work independently; the Bundle gives you both at a discount.

Can I get a refund?

Refund terms will be published alongside checkout. In the current preview, no payments are processed, so no refunds apply.

Do you offer group or team pricing?

Group access is on the roadmap but not open yet. For organisations wanting multiple seats, contact us and we will coordinate manually in the meantime.

What if I can't pay?

We're building partner access through incubators, universities and funders. If you cannot afford the course, write to us and we'll find a route for you.

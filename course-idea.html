The starter course for entrepreneurs

Idea into Impact: From an Idea to a Launch Plan

From an idea to a first plan you can act on · 5 modules · 15 lessons · about 6 hours

For people with an idea and nothing built yet: a project, a nonprofit, a social business or a cooperative. In five short modules you test the idea with real people, shape a small first version, start safely and plan your first 90 days. You finish with a Launch Plan you can share.

The same five steps as the organisation courses, kept simple: Clarify, Organise, Prove & Show, Protect, Sustain & Run.

Every lesson has a short explanation, three founders’ answers to learn from and a guided exercise that saves into your Launch Plan. Nothing assumes you already have an organisation, a team or a budget.

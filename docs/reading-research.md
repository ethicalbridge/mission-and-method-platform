# Reading research and selection method

## Scope

The Planning System contains 37 taught lessons across eight modules. Every taught lesson has at least two matched readings. The September 2026 review produced 75 lesson-to-source placements drawn from 35 distinct documents or guidance pages.

## Selection criteria

Sources were selected when their substantive scope matched the lesson rather than merely sharing a broad topic label. Preference was given to:

- official guidance and standards from public institutions and regulators;
- primary material from the organisation responsible for a framework;
- established evaluation and nonprofit research organisations with a transparent practice base;
- open resources a learner can read without buying a textbook;
- practical documents that complement, test or qualify the course exercise.

Each source includes a short note explaining why it is relevant. Regulatory guidance names its jurisdiction. The library does not imply that guidance from England and Wales or the wider UK applies elsewhere.

## Coverage

1. Strategic Foundation: mission, vision, values, strategic choices and objectives.
2. Theory of Change: causal pathways, problem framing, results levels, indicators, assumptions and contribution.
3. Team, Structure & Coordination: governance, decision rights, delivery roles, scheduling and induction.
4. Organisation Internal Systems: end-to-end services, user needs, inclusive communication, ethical representation, monitoring and learning.
5. Policies & Procedures: risk-led policy priorities, usable controls, finance, privacy and safeguarding.
6. Strategy to Action: work breakdown, accountable ownership, dependencies, milestones and resource-aware scheduling.
7. Business Model: customer value, delivery design, costs, cash flow and hypothesis testing.
8. Fundraising, Sponsors & Partnerships: evidence-based funding strategy, prospect and partner diligence, ethical cases for support and stewardship.

## Maintenance

External publishers can revise or move resources. Local tests enforce reading coverage and metadata for every taught lesson. External destinations were checked during this review; automated requests to some publishers were blocked even though the pages were available through normal browsing. Recheck the library before a major course release and replace sources that have moved, become paywalled or are superseded.

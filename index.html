QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

What to know before you begin.

What's live now, and what's coming?

The consultancy is open today: one-to-one work with Julieta. The Courses, Impact Tools, Evidence Library and Legal Templates are in build and open through 2027. Each one has a Notify me link in the "What we do" section above.

How do we start?

Send a short brief about where you are. We meet online for a free 30-minute discovery call. Within a week I come back with a tailored proposal: scope, timeline, price. If it fits, we agree a Terms of Reference and start. Start the brief →

Who do you work with?

Founders with an idea they want to turn into an organisation, and small NGOs or social enterprises (typically 2 to 10 people) that need their foundations to catch up with the work.

What does the consultancy cost?

By proposal, after the discovery call. The scope is modular (built around five foundation steps) and the price scales with how many steps you need help with. Fair rates by country.

Is Method into Impact a team or just Julieta?

Just Julieta. One practitioner, one point of contact, no handoffs. The person preparing your work is the person you're talking to.

Where are you based and who can you work with?

Clients are global. Julieta is based on UTC+7 and works in English or Spanish. Calls are scheduled around your time zone.

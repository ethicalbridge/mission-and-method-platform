QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

What to know before you begin.

What is included in the course price?

Every lesson, example, exercise, downloadable resource and Excel workbook across all five courses — plus Your Organisation workspace, the Evidence Library and the Legal Templates library. See the Pricing & packages page for current pricing and bundles.

Do I need to subscribe to software?

No. The Software Suite is an optional subscription. Each tool works independently. The course alone does not grant a Suite subscription; the optional launch bundle explicitly includes both.

How is my progress saved?

Drafts and completion are stored in this browser. Use Your Organisation to export a backup and import it on another device. This preview does not sync to an account.

Can I preview before I buy?

Yes. Module 0 (Start Here) is free. Open the first lesson, describe your organisation and run a baseline health check — no card required.

Who is this for?

Early-stage founders, social entrepreneurs, NGOs, community projects, emerging charities, impact startups and anyone building a purpose-led organisation. You don't need a finished plan, a team or previous strategy experience.

The method

Eight modules. Three phases. One strategy pack you actually use.

Each module explains the concepts, shows real examples and guides you through an exercise. Finish it, and that piece of your organisation is done — ready to share with your board, team or funders.

01 Clarify

Set the direction

Define the vision, mission, values and strategic objectives that guide every decision.

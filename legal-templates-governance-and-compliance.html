Governance & compliance

Governance and compliance documents help leaders oversee decisions, keep records and check that required duties are being met.

Registers, checklists, terms of reference and assessments with worked examples, so your board and team can see who decides what and whether duties are being met.

Templates, not legal advice

Each template is a general starting point written for small purpose-led organisations in different countries. Before you adopt, sign or publish one, adapt it to your organisation and have a qualified adviser check it against the law where you operate, your funders’ conditions and your sector’s rules.

Method into Impact does not guarantee that a template is complete, current, suitable or enforceable for your circumstances, and using one does not create an adviser–client relationship. You are responsible for getting advice and approving the final document. Nothing here limits rights or duties that cannot lawfully be excluded.

How each template works

  1. Fill in the yellow fields with your organisation’s details: names, roles, amounts, time limits and countries.
  2. Work through the orange “Adapt this” boxes. Each one explains a decision to make and gives options and country notes. Delete the box when you are done.
  3. Cut what does not apply, so the document stays proportionate to your size.
  4. Ask a qualified adviser in your country to check the final wording. The Legal Review Checklist helps you prepare.
  5. Approve, date and share it. Record the approval, set a review date and make sure people know it exists.

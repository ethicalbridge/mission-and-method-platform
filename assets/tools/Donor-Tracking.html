Using the tracking register

Keep one record per donor or sponsor and one gift row per actual receipt. Record pledges in notes until received. Choose a relationship owner, set the next action and due date, and log substantive interactions so another team member can understand the history. Amounts are summed only when all gifts use the same currency; mixed-currency records display a warning instead of an inaccurate total.

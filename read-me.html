Your first steps

Begin with the idea you have.

  1. Open the Planning System and start with Module 1, Purpose. You do not need previous strategy experience.

  2. Read the explanation, compare the examples and consider the reflection question.

  3. Draft your answers. They save in this browser as you type. Complete the quality check, then choose Save & continue.

  4. At the end of each module, review your output. Earlier answers remain available as context in later course lessons.

  5. Visit Your Organisation to download your strategy pack or export a backup. Import that backup to move course work to another device.

You can complete the course without subscribing to software. The course Excel files are included resources; they are awaiting upload in this preview.

Use organisation-level planning notes here. Keep confidential participant, financial and personnel records in an appropriate secure system.

Strategic Foundation

Module 2

Theory of Change

Explain how your work could contribute to change. Work backwards from the future you seek, then check the pathway forwards. Your map is a reasoned hypothesis to test with people affected, not proof that an intervention works.

2.1 Understanding Theory of Change · 2 readings

Lesson focus: A Theory of Change makes your reasoning explicit: if we do this work, why might a particular change follow? It includes the context, people, sequence of changes and assumptions connecting them.

Module 09Financial Management11 lessons+

Set up money management well from the start, so you can make good decisions, protect resources and earn the trust of funders, customers, members and partners. This module is practical, not an accounting qualification: it covers what founders need to budget, forecast, control, report and oversee finances in a new initiative, what will be required as you grow, and when to bring in a qualified accountant.

Module 10Legal, Compliance, Policies & Risk14 lessons+

Build your legal and compliance framework before you launch. Map the legal duties that will apply to you, work out which policies and controls you will actually need given the form you choose, your activities, geography, size, funding and risk profile, write procedures people can follow, keep policies alive as you grow, and manage risk with clear owners and escalation from the start. This module supports planning; legal duties depend on your jurisdiction, so check the rules where you are and obtain qualified local advice.

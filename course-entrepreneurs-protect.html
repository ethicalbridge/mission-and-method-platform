Module 10Legal, Compliance, Policies & Risk13 lessons+

Get your legal and compliance foundations right before you launch. The first lessons explain the legal duties that will apply to you, which policies and procedures a new initiative actually needs, and what each one is for, with examples from our bakery. They have no exercises: each one points you to templates you can adapt. The last lessons help you identify your risks and build a risk register with clear owners and escalation from the start. Legal duties depend on where you operate, so adapt every template to your local laws and have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before you use it.

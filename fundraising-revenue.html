Fundraising, Revenue & Sustainable Business Models

Every organisation needs a sustainable resource model, but the model depends on what kind of organisation you are. Nonprofits, charities and foundations usually raise funds from grants and donations; companies and social enterprises earn revenue from customers; hybrids combine both. Start with the foundations, then study the income streams that fit your model, and learn how to win, keep and report on support.
